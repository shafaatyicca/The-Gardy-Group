Auditing Your Website

A thorough website audit takes the lead within our suite of SEO services. We leave no stone unturned, meticulously scouring your site for areas that demand addition or enhancement.

Title Tags, Meta Descriptions, Page Titles, Page Copy, Alt Text

Title tags, meta descriptions, page titles, page copy, and alt text play a crucial role. Incorporating keywords into these areas boost your site's visibility and relevance on search engines. Optimize your content with the right search engine consulting, and watch your online presence soar!

URL Structure

Mastering the URL structure for SEO is crucial. Opt for real words with your keywords in URLs. Craft them to entice search engines, facilitating seamless site crawling based on URLs alone.

Navigation Structure

Is your website easily navigable by users and search engine robots? Optimize your navigation structure for simplicity, consistency, and organization. It's vital for SEO, usability, and conversion rates. A user-friendly website keeps visitors engaged and boosts your chances of success. So, make sure your navigation is on point!

Duplicate Copy Check

A duplicate copy can harm your website's ranking power. If you use the same content on multiple pages or other websites, swipe your stuff, it's time for action. Get that content re-written quickly to protect your SEO services for the website!

Backlink Review

Backlinks for your website are crucial, but you have to stay vigilant! Keep an eye on the pages linking back to you to ensure they're active and legit—no shady stuff allowed. Bad links can mess up your SEO game. And hey, don't forget to spy on your competitors' backlinks! You might find some new link buddies to boost your search engine rankings. Stay sharp and watch those backlinks!
